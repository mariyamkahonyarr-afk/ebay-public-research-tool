What this tool shows — and what it does not

Live listings only, from eBay Browse API in the same search. The All markets total is the sum across the 8 markets in the market map, not a count of unique products. Sales (est.) is eBay's estimated sold quantity from the item-detail call, for single-market searches only. Total Sales by Seller is the sum of those estimates for one seller in this search only. Chances of Success is a transparent tool score out of 100, NOT eBay data: Sales (est.) > 0 = 40 points, Feedback >=1000 = 25 / >=100 = 15 / >=10 = 5, Price <= search average = 20 / <= average x 1.2 = 10, Condition New/Ungraded/Factory Sealed = 15. Winning = 70% or more, Watch = 40-69%, Losing = under 40%. Without sales data a row cannot reach Winning, because sales are 40 of the 100 points. A failed sold call is never replaced with an invented number or a search total.
